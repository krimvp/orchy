# Ten days in Japan, late October

Two of us, mid-thirties, first time in Japan. We land in Tokyo on a Friday
morning and fly home from Osaka ten days later, so the trip has to end in the
west.

What we want:

- One day of nothing at all, somewhere quiet, halfway through.
- Ramen and a proper market breakfast. We will queue for good food.
- One temple day, not five. We tire of them.
- A day trip out of the city on foot, hills rather than a bus tour.
- Kyoto in autumn colour, if the timing works.

What we do not want:

- Anything that starts before 08:00.
- More than one long train journey in a day.
- A guided coach tour of any kind.

Budget: 4200 USD for the two of us on the ground. Flights are paid already.
We have a rail pass for seven of the ten days.
