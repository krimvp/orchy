Write the course to `CURRICULUM.md` in the working directory. Change no other
file.

The module authors planned the hours below, and a module computed whether they
fit. If `fits` is false, cut sessions until they do, weakest first, and list
what you cut in `cut`. Do not cut a checkpoint.

The file holds one section per week: what happens, how many hours, and the
checkpoint when the week ends one. Open it with the prerequisites and a line
on what the learner will be able to do at the end.

`weeks_written` is how many weeks the file covers.
