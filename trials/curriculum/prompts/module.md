The value this step holds is one module of the course: its name, its length in
weeks, and its outcome.

Fill in that module alone.

- `sessions` breaks it into sittings. `hours` is a number, and it must be
  honest: a later step adds these up against the hours the learner actually
  has.
- `doing` says what the learner does in that sitting. Reading counts only when
  something is produced from it.
- `project` is the one thing they build across the module.
- `checkpoint` is how they know they can move on, phrased as something they
  can attempt and fail.
