# Who is studying

I am a data analyst, six years in. I write SQL every day and Python when I
have to. I have never written a test, never used a debugger, and I do not
really know what happens between my script and the database.

I want to stop being the person who hands a notebook to an engineer and waits.
I would like to ship a small service myself, on a schedule, that other people
depend on, without being frightened of it.

Facts:

- Eight hours a week, realistically. Two evenings and a Saturday morning.
- I get bored by exercises that produce nothing. I finished none of the three
  online courses I started.
- I have a laptop, a company AWS account I can use for small things, and no
  budget.
- I already know pandas, SQL well, and enough git to commit and push.
- Statistics I am fine with. It is the engineering I am missing.
