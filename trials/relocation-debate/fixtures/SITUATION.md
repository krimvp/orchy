# Two offers and a house

I am a backend engineer, eleven years in, currently in Manchester on 78k GBP.
I own a flat here with about 190k left on the mortgage; it would rent for
roughly 1,150 a month. My partner is a nurse and can transfer within the EU
with some paperwork, but not instantly.

Offer A: Lisbon, 68k EUR, a payments company, four days in the office. They
pay relocation up to 8k and will sponsor the visa paperwork for both of us.
The team is twelve people and the product is two years old.

Offer B: Berlin, 92k EUR, an established logistics firm, two days in the
office. No relocation money. The team I would join is forty people and the
codebase is nineteen years old. My would-be manager left the interview loop
halfway through and was replaced.

Staying: I have been passed over for a staff role twice here, the second time
last month. My manager says the next cycle is "likely" but has said that
before. I like the people. The work has been the same for three years.

What I care about, roughly in order: doing work I am not bored by, being able
to buy a home eventually, my partner not resenting the move, and living
somewhere with light in winter. My mother is 71 and lives in Leeds; she is
well now. I have about 40k in savings and no children.
