Three analysts read the incident through three lenses. Their findings are below.

Turn them into actions.

- Each action is a thing someone does, not a value someone holds. "Be more
  careful" is not an action.
- `kind` says whether it prevents the failure, detects it sooner, limits the
  damage, or speeds the response. Cover at least three of the four.
- `owner_role` names a role, never a person.
- `not_doing` names what the team should decide against, and why. A postmortem
  that only adds work is a postmortem nobody finishes.
