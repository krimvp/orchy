# Stopping work in fourteen months

We are 61 and 63. The younger of us has a public pension of 19,000 a year from
67. State pensions come later and we are not counting on the full amount.

The pot is 890,000, of which 610,000 is in a sheltered account and 280,000 is
taxable. The house is worth about 420,000 and is paid off.

We think we need 52,000 a year to live the way we want to, and we could get by
on 41,000 if we had to. The first three years we want more, around 58,000,
because we intend to travel while we still can.

What we will not do:

- We will not move house before we are 75. Our daughter and the grandchildren
  are four streets away.
- Neither of us will go back to full-time work. Part-time consulting, maybe
  two days a week, for a couple of years, is possible for one of us.
- We will not put the pot into anything we cannot explain to each other.

What frightens us: a bad first five years, and finding out at 78 that we should
have spent less at 66.
