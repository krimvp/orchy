# Where we stand

I am 34, my partner is 36. We both work, and between us we can put aside about
2,300 USD a month without noticing it. We have 61,000 USD saved: 48,000 in a
broad index fund and 13,000 sitting in cash earning almost nothing.

We want to stop needing a salary in about 25 years. We think that takes around
1.4 million. We are not trying to retire early and sail somewhere.

Things that matter to us:

- We will not watch a screen. If a plan needs attention more than twice a year
  it is the wrong plan for us.
- We have been through 2022 and did not sell. We can sit through a fall, but a
  50% drawdown would test us.
- No individual stocks. We are not going to pick them and we know it.
- We would like some property exposure without becoming landlords.
- 20,000 of the cash is our emergency fund and must not be invested.
- Fees annoy us more than they probably should.

We are in the US and both have employer plans with a match, which we already
take in full.
