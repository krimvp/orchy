# The household, and the week ahead

Two adults and a nine-year-old. One adult works late on Tuesdays and Thursdays
and is not home before 20:30. The child eats at 18:00 whatever happens.

Friday is pizza, always, and it comes from the shop on the corner. Saturday we
eat at my sister's. So neither needs planning.

What we will not eat: no shellfish at all, one of us reacts. The child will not
touch mushrooms and we have stopped fighting about it. We are trying to eat
less red meat, twice a week at most.

Monday is a football night, so dinner has to be on the table by 18:15.
Wednesday is the only evening we all sit down together and we like that to be
worth it. Sunday there is time to cook properly and we usually make something
that gives us lunches.

Already in the cupboard: rice, pasta, tinned tomatoes, olive oil, soy sauce,
onions, garlic, plain flour, stock cubes, frozen peas.

We shop once, on Sunday morning. Budget is about 120 for the week's dinners.
The oven is small and the freezer is full.
