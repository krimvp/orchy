Write the week to `WEEK.md` in the working directory. Change no other file.

- A section for the week: one line per night, the dish, and the minutes.
- Then the shopping list exactly as the module merged it, by aisle. Do not
  re-sort it, add to it, or drop a line from it.
- Then a short section on what to cook ahead on the quietest night, using the
  leftovers the dinners named.

`nights_written` is how many nights the file covers.
