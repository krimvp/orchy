The value this step holds is one night: the day, the minutes available, and
what makes it different.

Choose the dinner for that night alone.

- `minutes` must be at or under what that night allows, including the washing
  up.
- `ingredients` names everything the dish needs, with a quantity for the
  household and the aisle it is found in. A later step merges these into one
  shopping list, so write the aisle as a plain word: produce, dairy, meat,
  fish, dry goods, frozen, other.
- Leave out what the earlier step listed as already on hand.
- `leftovers` says what is left and what it becomes, or "none".

Honour every avoidance the earlier step listed. Read `KITCHEN.md` if you need
the detail. Write no file.
