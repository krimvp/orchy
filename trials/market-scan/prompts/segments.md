Divide the market for {{ product }} in {{ market }} into segments that buy
differently, not into sizes of the same buyer.

- `name` is short and distinct, because a reader will compare them.
- `pain` is what that segment does today instead, and what it costs them.
- `pays` says who signs, out of which budget, and roughly what that budget
  bears in a year.

`buyer` names the one segment that would buy first, and nothing more.
