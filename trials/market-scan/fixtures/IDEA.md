# Scheduling for small physiotherapy clinics

I am a physiotherapist who taught myself to code. I ran a three-room clinic for
six years and the scheduling software we used cost 240 a month, crashed on
Mondays, and could not handle a therapist who works across two sites.

The idea: booking, reminders, and rescheduling built for clinics with one to
six practitioners. Text reminders that actually reduce no-shows, an owner who
can move an appointment from their phone between patients, and an export their
accountant will accept.

What I know from running one:

- No-shows are 12-18% and every one is 55 to 70 in lost revenue.
- Most clinics this size are on one of two old systems, or on paper and a
  shared calendar. Switching is terrifying because the diary is the business.
- Insurers and the health service both send referrals in formats nobody parses.
- The owner is also the person seeing patients. Nobody has an hour to onboard.
- Clinics talk to each other constantly. One good reference in a town matters
  more than any advertising.

What I do not know: whether they would pay for a new one, whether the two
incumbents would simply fix their apps, and whether I can sell anything at all.

I have nine months of savings and no co-founder.
