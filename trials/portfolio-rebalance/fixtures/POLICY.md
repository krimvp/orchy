# What we decided when we were calm

We rebalance at most twice a year, in March and September, and only when a
holding is more than five points away from its target. We do not rebalance
because the news is loud.

We sell in a taxable account only as a last resort. The 401k and the Roth are
where trades should happen, because nothing is owed on them.

We have 24,000 of new money to put in this year, in four quarterly lots. New
money goes to whatever is furthest below target. If new money alone closes the
gap, we place no other trade.

The taxable global equity position holds lots bought in 2019 with large gains.
Two lots were bought in the last eleven months and we should not sell those.

We do not hold individual stocks and we are not going to start. We have never
harvested a loss and would need to be walked through it.
