A module read the holdings and worked out the trades. They are below, and they
are not up for discussion: do not recompute them, and do not propose different
amounts.

Read `HOLDINGS.json` and `POLICY.md` in the working directory.

- `summary` says in three sentences what drifted, why, and what the trades do
  about it.
- `order_of_operations` says what to do first, second, and third, so that
  nothing is sold before the cash that could have been used instead.
- `watch_out` names what the arithmetic cannot see: which account a trade
  lands in, a wash sale, a settlement delay, a holding that cannot be traded
  in small amounts.

Write no file.
