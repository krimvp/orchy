The module says a rebalance is needed. Read `HOLDINGS.json` for which account
each holding sits in, and `POLICY.md` for what the household has decided.

- `cheaper_route` names the way to reach the same allocation while realising
  less tax: rebalancing inside the sheltered accounts, directing the next
  contributions, or waiting for a date the policy names.
- `cost_of_doing_nothing` says what happens if the drift is left alone for
  another year.

This is not tax advice, and you do not know their bracket. Say what to check
rather than what to owe. Write no file.
