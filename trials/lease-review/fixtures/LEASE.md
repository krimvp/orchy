# Residential tenancy agreement (draft)

**1. Term.** The tenancy runs for 24 months from 1 September, and cannot be
ended by the Tenant before the end of the term.

**2. Rent.** Rent is 1,950 per month, payable on the 1st. Rent increases by 8%
on each anniversary of the commencement date.

**3. Deposit.** The Tenant pays a deposit of three months' rent. The Landlord
returns it within 90 days of the end of the tenancy, less any deduction the
Landlord considers reasonable.

**4. Early termination.** If the Tenant leaves before the end of the term, the
Tenant remains liable for the rent for the remainder of the term, and pays a
re-letting fee equal to two months' rent.

**5. Repairs.** The Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance
inside the property, including appliances, plumbing, and the boiler,
regardless of cause or age.

**6. Access.** The Landlord or the Landlord's agent may enter the property at
any reasonable time without prior notice to inspect its condition.

**7. Guests.** No person other than the named Tenant may stay overnight for
more than two nights in any month without the Landlord's written consent.

**8. Alterations.** The Tenant may not hang pictures, paint, or attach
anything to a wall. Any such mark is charged at the Landlord's cost of repair.

**9. Assignment.** The Tenant may not assign or sublet. The Landlord may
assign this agreement to any party without notice to the Tenant.

**10. Costs.** In any dispute, the Tenant pays the Landlord's legal costs
regardless of outcome.

**11. Notices.** Notices to the Tenant are validly served by leaving them at
the property, whether or not the Tenant receives them.
