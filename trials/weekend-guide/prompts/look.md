Search the web for what is actually open in {{ city }} in {{ month }}, for
{{ with_whom }}.

The point of this step is currency, not taste. A guide written four years ago
is worse than nothing.

- `open_now` holds at least four places or events, each with the URL that says
  it is open, and one line on why it suits who is going.
- `closed_or_changed` names anything a stale guide would send them to that has
  since closed, moved, or now needs booking.
- Prefer an official site, a municipal page, or the venue itself.

Write no file.
